Early Blight

Alternaria solani

A pervasive fungal disease characterized by concentric bullseye target rings on mature lower leaves, rapidly escalating up the plant canopy under warm, alternating wet and dry conditions.

🔬 Test Leaf Specimen in AI Studio

🔍 Diagnostic Symptoms & Visual Markers

  • Circular to oval brown-black spots displaying distinct concentric target rings (bullseye pattern).
  • Chlorotic yellow halos surrounding expanding leaf lesions, leading to leaf senescence and drop.
  • Defoliation starting from older, shaded lower leaves and progressing upwards.
  • Dark, sunken, leathery lesions near fruit stems causing severe fruit drop.

Visual Morphology Rule: Concentric circular rings with yellow halos. Lacks the feathery margins of Rose Black Spot and greasy rot of Late Blight.

🧬 Causes & Biology

Ascomycete fungus Alternaria solani. Conidia spores overwinter in diseased crop debris and volunteer solanaceous weeds.

🚜 Cultural Prevention & Sanitation

  • 3-year crop rotation away from solanaceous crops.
  • Drip irrigation to keep foliar canopy completely dry.
  • Mulch soil to prevent rain-splash spore transmission.
  • Prune lower foliage 30 cm above ground for air ventilation.

🌱 Certified Organic Remedies

Bacillus subtilis bio-fungicide, copper octanoate, and potassium bicarbonate washes.

🧪 Chemical Protocols & FRAC Fungicides

Chlorothalonil (FRAC M05), Azoxystrobin (FRAC 11), Difenoconazole (FRAC 3). Rotate FRAC modes to prevent resistance.

⚠️ Stewardship: Always rotate single-site systemic fungicides with multi-site contact protectants to avoid fungal resistance.
