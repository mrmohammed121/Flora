Plant Disease Identification Directory

Explore scientific agronomic guides for common crop pathologies. Learn visual symptoms, causes, organic solutions, and chemical treatment protocols.

Tomato, Potato, Eggplant

Early Blight

Alternaria solani

A pervasive fungal disease characterized by concentric bullseye target rings on mature lower leaves, rapidly escalating up the plant canopy under warm, alternating wet and dry conditions.

 Tomato, Potato

Late Blight

Phytophthora infestans

The devastating water-mold responsible for the Irish Potato Famine. Produces aggressive water-soaked canopy rot, blackened stems, and white sporulation under cool, saturated humidity.

 Tomato, Bell Pepper

Bacterial Spot

Xanthomonas perforans

A bacterial disease that thrives in warm, rainy weather, creating numerous small, water-soaked angular spots that tear into a distinctive shot-hole pattern.

 Rose (Rosa spp.)

Rose Black Spot

Diplocarpon rosae

The primary foliar disease of garden roses. Characterized by dark circular spots with distinct feathery, fibrillose edges, progressing to yellowing and severe defoliation.

 Apple, Crabapple

Apple Scab

Venturia inaequalis

The most economically significant apple disease worldwide, causing velvety olive-green to black scabby lesions on leaves and corky cracks on mature fruit.

 Apple, Pear

Apple Black Rot

Botryosphaeria obtusa

A triple-threat orchard pathogen that causes frogeye leaf spots, sunken wood cankers on branches, and firm black mummified fruit rot.

 Orange, Lemon, Lime, Grapefruit

Citrus Greening (HLB)

Candidatus Liberibacter asiaticus

Huanglongbing (HLB), the most destructive disease of citrus worldwide. A phloem-limited bacterium vectored by psyllid insects, causing asymmetric leaf mottling and bitter fruit.

 Cucumber, Squash, Melon, Zucchini

Powdery Mildew

Podosphaera xanthii

A widespread fungal disease that coats leaf surfaces in white, talcum-powder-like patches, leading to leaf chlorosis, premature drying, and reduced photosynthesis.

 Cucumber, Cantaloupe, Watermelon

Downy Mildew

Pseudoperonospora cubensis

A fast-moving oomycete disease causing distinct angular, vein-bounded yellow chlorotic patches on upper leaf surfaces, with purplish-gray down underneath.
