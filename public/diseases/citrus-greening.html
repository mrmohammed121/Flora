Citrus Greening (HLB)

Candidatus Liberibacter asiaticus

Huanglongbing (HLB), the most destructive disease of citrus worldwide. A phloem-limited bacterium vectored by psyllid insects, causing asymmetric leaf mottling and bitter fruit.

🔬 Test Leaf Specimen in AI Studio

🔍 Diagnostic Symptoms & Visual Markers

  • Asymmetric blotchy yellow mottle on leaves crossing veins irregularly.
  • Yellowing of individual branches while remainder of canopy stays green (yellow shoot).
  • Small, lopsided fruit that stays green at the stylar end and tastes bitter.
  • Feeder root dieback and progressive tree mortality.

Visual Morphology Rule: Asymmetric blotchy mottle crossing veins unevenly. Lopsided fruit with green stylar ends and dark aborted seeds.

🧬 Causes & Biology

Phloem-restricted bacterium CLas, vectored from tree to tree by the Asian Citrus Psyllid (Diaphorina citri).

🚜 Cultural Prevention & Sanitation

  • Plant certified disease-free nursery trees grown in screened structures.
  • Scout bi-weekly for Asian citrus psyllid nymphs and adults.
  • Immediately remove and destroy confirmed HLB-infected trees.

🌱 Certified Organic Remedies

Horticultural mineral oils; Tamarixia radiata parasitoid wasp biological control.

🧪 Chemical Protocols & FRAC Fungicides

Systemic neonicotinoids (Imidacloprid) soil drench on young trees; foliar pyrethroid flushes.

⚠️ Stewardship: No cure exists once infected. Management relies on psyllid insect vector control and tree sanitation.
